### AA1's step list

Written 20 October 2026, before the owner's round, so that what the round covered is on record: AA6 asks that question of step 4's keyboard round because its steps were not written down. Safari with VoiceOver on the Mac, on the published site; a fix is confirmed on its pull request's deploy preview, behind the sign-in, as step 4's were. One check at a time, as step 4 was run: the owner does a check and reports what he heard; the assistant puts the fix, makes it on the owner's word, and names what to confirm before the next check. Each check names its page and address, and names controls, tables and disclosures by their visible words. The counts are from the build of 19 October 2026. On every page, open every disclosure, "More about this figure" among them, and read the page from top to foot with VO+Right, then list its headings, tables, links and landmarks with the rotor. Report, for each check: anything read twice, read in pieces, not read at all, read without the words that give it meaning, or read in an order that is not the page's; a heading, table, list or link the rotor does not list; and anything that surprised him. What step 4 heard is not repeated: the State Pension page's table, and the four calculators' results, charts and messages, and the rule comparison's copied link, as its paragraph records; the checks below name the parts of those pages it did not reach. Two things judged by other rows will be heard on the way, and what is heard of them is reported under those rows, not this one: the "Out of date" note that comes with a wrong or half-typed figure (AA4), and whether a choice that changes other fields makes the change clear (AA3).
